# Open films

The full films retain their end credits. The player names the actual film and credits the Blender Foundation under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). The three shelf thumbnails are frames from the credited films.

| Film | Playback source | Credits and licence |
|---|---|---|
| Big Buck Bunny | [GARR MP4](https://kodi.mirror.garr.it/demo-files/BBB/bbb_sunflower_2160p_30fps_normal.mp4) on web and [Mux HLS](https://test-streams.mux.dev/x36xhzz/url_6/193039199_mp4_h264_aac_hq_7.m3u8) on native | [Blender Peach project](https://peach.blender.org/about/) |
| Sintel | [Internet Archive MP4](https://archive.org/download/Sintel/sintel-2048-stereo_512kb.mp4), H.264/AAC, 564 × 240, 14:48 | [Blender Durian project licence](https://durian.blender.org/sharing/) |
| Tears of Steel | [Internet Archive MP4](https://archive.org/download/Tears-of-Steel/tears_of_steel_720p.mp4), H.264/AAC, 862 × 360, 12:14 | [Blender Mango project licence](https://mango.blender.org/about/) |

The Home hero and Open films shelf use modified excerpts from approximately 00:30 to 00:40. The excerpts are bundled as resized H.264/AAC assets, muted by default. The film sources and CC BY 3.0 credits above apply to each excerpt.
