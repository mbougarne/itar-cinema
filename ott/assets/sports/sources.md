# Cinema sports sources

The fixture archive includes the 104 fixtures and results from [OpenFootball’s World Cup 2026 JSON](https://github.com/openfootball/worldcup.json/blob/0c4b73b3c1fbb877e8274eb321b1ec4d9220a3a4/2026/worldcup.json). The repository licenses its football data under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Cinema keeps teams, venue, round, kickoff converted to UTC, final score and penalty shootout scores. The archive provides historical results. It has no live-state signal.

*Men’s triple jump*, Gwangju 2015, by FISUTV, International University Sports Federation. The [Wikimedia Commons file page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Men%27s_Triple_Jump.webm) includes the original upload and a verified [CC BY 3.0 licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Cinema converted the 36-second VP8/Vorbis source to H.264/AAC MP4, scaled it to 854 × 480 and extracted a thumbnail. It plays separately from the World Cup results.
